Meet your dentist

Dr. Akash Agarwal

Dentist & Founder · BDS, Seema Dental College & Hospital, Rishikesh

Dr. Akash Agarwal leads Family Dental Clinic in Kotdwar with a simple belief: every patient — from a child's first check-up to a grandparent's new dentures — deserves unhurried, honest and pain-free care.

A BDS graduate of Seema Dental College & Hospital, Rishikesh — Uttarakhand's NAAC-accredited dental college — he explains every option in plain language, shows you what he sees, and recommends only the treatment you actually need. The clinic follows strict sterilisation protocols and uses modern equipment so that visits are quick, comfortable and safe.

Scaling, Cleaning & Gum Care

Professional cleaning removes tartar, stops bleeding gums and keeps your breath fresh.

What is scaling, cleaning & gum care?

Even with good brushing, tartar builds up in places your brush can't reach. Professional scaling and polishing removes it, helping prevent gum disease and bad breath.

For bleeding or receding gums we offer deep cleaning and gum treatment, with a home-care plan to keep the results.

How it works

  1. 01

    Gum check

    We measure gum health and look for problem areas.

  2. 02

    Scaling

    Ultrasonic instruments remove tartar above and below the gum line.

  3. 03

    Polishing

    Teeth are polished smooth to slow new build-up.

  4. 04

    Home plan

    Personalised brushing and flossing advice.

Aftercare

  • Mild sensitivity can occur for a day or two.
  • Return every six months for a check-up and cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

Does scaling damage the enamel?+

No. Scaling removes hardened deposits, not tooth structure. Teeth may feel different afterwards simply because the tartar is gone.

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?+

Most people benefit from a professional cleaning every six months.

You may need this if you have…

  • Bleeding gums while brushing
  • Bad breath
  • Visible yellow or brown deposits
  • Routine six-monthly check-up

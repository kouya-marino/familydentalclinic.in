Crowns & Bridges

Strengthen broken or root-canal-treated teeth with a crown, or fill a gap with a fixed bridge.

What is crowns & bridges?

A crown is a tooth-shaped cap that covers a weak, broken or heavily filled tooth, restoring its strength and appearance. A bridge uses crowns on neighbouring teeth to hold a replacement tooth across a gap.

We offer metal-ceramic, full-ceramic and zirconia options so you can choose the right balance of strength, appearance and budget.

How it works

  1. 01

    Tooth preparation

    The tooth is gently shaped to make room for the crown.

  2. 02

    Impression & shade

    An accurate impression and colour match are taken.

  3. 03

    Temporary crown

    A temporary crown protects the tooth while the final one is made.

  4. 04

    Fitting

    The final crown or bridge is checked for bite and cemented in place.

Aftercare

  • Avoid very hard or sticky food on a temporary crown.
  • Clean under a bridge daily with floss threaders or an interdental brush.

Frequently asked questions

Which crown material is best?+

Zirconia and full-ceramic crowns look the most natural; metal-ceramic crowns are strong and economical. Dr. Akash Agarwal will recommend an option based on the tooth's position and your bite.

How long does a crown take?+

Usually two visits: one to prepare the tooth and one to fit the final crown.

You may need this if you have…

  • A tooth after root canal treatment
  • A cracked, worn or broken tooth
  • A large old filling that is failing
  • One or two missing teeth (bridge)

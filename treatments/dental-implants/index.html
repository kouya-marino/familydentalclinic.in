Dental Implants

A permanent, natural-looking replacement for missing teeth — a titanium root topped with a custom crown.

Call Us +91 94105 95800WhatsApp

What is dental implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone and acts like a natural tooth root. A custom crown, bridge or denture is then fixed on top.

Implants don't depend on the neighbouring teeth for support, help preserve jawbone, and let you chew and smile with confidence.

How it works

  1. 01

    Consultation & planning

    Examination and X-rays to check bone height, gum health and the ideal implant position.

  2. 02

    Implant placement

    The implant is placed under local anaesthesia in a short, precise procedure.

  3. 03

    Healing

    Over the following weeks the implant bonds with the bone.

  4. 04

    Abutment & crown

    A connector and a custom-shaded crown are fitted to complete your new tooth.

Aftercare

  • Stick to soft food on that side for the first few days.
  • Brush gently around the area and keep your follow-up appointments.
  • Avoid smoking — it significantly increases the risk of implant failure.

Frequently asked questions

How long do dental implants last?+

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, implants can last for many years — often decades. The crown on top may need replacement over time due to normal wear.

Am I too old for dental implants?+

Age alone is rarely a barrier. What matters most is your general health, gum health and the amount of jawbone available.

Does getting an implant hurt?+

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia. Most patients report only mild discomfort for a few days afterwards.

You may need this if you have…

  • One or more missing teeth
  • Loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • A tooth that cannot be saved and must be removed
  • Adults with good general health and enough jawbone (or who can have bone grafting)
